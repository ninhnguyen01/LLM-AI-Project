"What is AGI (Artificial General Intelligence)?"

-theoretical AI research that attempts to create software with human-like intelligence and the ability to self-teach. 

Strong AI compared with weak AI 
- Strong AI is full artificial intelligence, or AGI, capable of performing tasks with human cognitive levels despite having little background knowledge. 
Example: Cortana from the video game Halo

- weak AI or narrow AI are AI systems limited to computing specifications, algorithms, and specific tasks they are designed for.
Example: ChatGPT
